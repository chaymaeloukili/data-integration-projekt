# Permission from the FFA

| | |
|---|---|
| Request sent | 22.10.2026, by Chaymae Loukili to the market research department of the FFA (German Federal Film Board) |
| Answer received | 27.10.2026, from FFA market research |
| About | Numbers from "Das Kinojahr" (2000–2025), "Kinobesucher\*innen 2025" and "Vom Film zum Kinoerlebnis" (2026) |

## Result

- **Use allowed:** The numbers published by the FFA may be used, combined in a new table and **published with the source**.
- **How to cite**, as recommended by the FFA: name the FFA and the publication, if possible with the year. For example:
  - Source: Filmförderungsanstalt (FFA), Das Kinojahr 2025
  - for several years: Source: Filmförderungsanstalt (FFA), Das Kinojahr, various years
- **Tables:** According to the FFA, most published tables can be imported from the PDFs into Excel with Power Query.
- **Extra data:** The FFA sent a PowerPoint file with the numbers of cinemas and arthouse cinemas for 2016–2025. The numbers are saved as a table in [data/raw/ffa/ffa_cinemas_arthouse_2016_2025.csv](../../data/raw/ffa/ffa_cinemas_arthouse_2016_2025.csv).
- **Interviews in "Vom Film zum Kinoerlebnis":** 77 interviews in total: 10 experts, 57 consumers in Germany and 10 consumers in France.

## What is not uploaded

The FFA files themselves (PDFs, PowerPoint) and the pictures (film photos) from the reports are not uploaded to this repository. They can be downloaded from the FFA website (links in [the source overview](README.md)).

The full email is kept by Chaymae Loukili and can be shown if needed.
