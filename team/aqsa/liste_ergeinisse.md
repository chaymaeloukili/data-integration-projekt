# Timeline: Key Events Affecting Cinema Attendance 

The list collects key events that my habe influenced cinema visits in Germany, such as the launch of streaming services, the COVID-19 lockdowns and the reopening of cinemas. It helps us place changes in our data in the time and define meaningful periods for our analysis. 

## Events

| Date | Event | Relevance for our project | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2014-02-26 | Amazon Prime Instant Video launches in Germany | First major streaming service in Germany. From here on, we can check whether cinema attendance changes in the streaming era. | [mobiFlip (German)](https://www.mobiflip.de/?p=447036) |
| 2014-09-16 | Netflix launches in Germany | More competition between streaming platforms expands the entertainment offer at home. We check whether cinema attendance declines afterwards. | [prisma.de (German)](https://www.prisma.de/news/Netflix-in-Deutschland-gestartet,1244858) |
| 2020-03-16 | Federal and state governments decide to close cinemas (first lockdown) | Start of the COVID-19 slump: we expect a sharp drop in cinema attendance from March 2020. We mark 2020 and 2021 separately in our analysis. | [German Federal Government (German)](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/service/archiv/leitlinien-zum-kampf-gegen-die-corona-epidemie-vom-16-03-2020-1730942) |
| 2020-03-24 | Disney+ launches in Germany | Launch falls into the period of cinema closures due to COVID-19. We check whether people got used to streaming during this time and go to the cinema less often afterwards. | [inFranken.de (German)](https://www.infranken.de/ratgeber/verbraucher/schnaeppchen/disney-startet-heute-in-deutschland-alle-filme-und-serien-im-ueberblick-gratis-fuer-telekom-kunden-art-4979431) |
| 2020-11-02 | Second lockdown: cinemas have to close again | Cinemas stay closed until summer 2021. Explains why 2021 is so low in our data. | [HDF Kino (German)](https://www.hdf-kino.de/wp-content/uploads/2020-10-28__Pressemitteilung_HDF_Erneuter-Lockdown-ist-für-die-Kinos-existenziell.pdf) |
| 2021-07-01 | Nationwide reopening of cinemas | Start of the recovery. From here on, we measure how quickly attendance comes back. | [HDF Kino (German)](https://www.hdf-kino.de/wp-content/uploads/21-06-28_Pressemitteilung_HDF_Wiedereröffnung_Länderregelungen.pdf) |
| 2nd half of 2021 | 2G/2G+ rules, capacity limits and regional closures | The recovery is slowed down. 2021 is therefore not a normal cinema year and is marked separately. | [HDF Kino (German)](https://www.hdf-kino.de/wp-content/uploads/22-01-14-Pressemitteilung_HDF_VdF_Kinomarkt_2021_2022.pdf) |
| 2023-05-02 – 2023-11-09 | Writers' and actors' strikes in Hollywood | Film releases are postponed. May explain a decline in 2024 due to missing blockbusters. | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Hollywood_labor_disputes) |
| 2023-07-20 | "Barbie" and "Oppenheimer" open on the same day ("Barbenheimer") | Symbol of cinema as an event. Oppenheimer was also shot on film, so it fits the analog trend as well. | [FILMSTARTS (German)](https://www.filmstarts.de/nachrichten/1000041655.html) |
| 2024 | Fewer tickets sold than in the record year 2023 | Setback after the recovery. We check whether missing blockbusters (consequences of the strikes) explain this. | [Nordkurier (German)](https://www.nordkurier.de/regional/brandenburg/filmfoerderchef-achterbahnfahrt-fuer-deutsche-kinos-3193468) |
| 2025 | 91.9 million tickets sold, clearly more than in 2024 | New recovery. Most recent data point for our analysis. | [Berliner Zeitung (German)](https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/news/deutsches-kino-erlebt-starkes-jahr-2025-kanu-des-manitu-treibt-marktanteil-auf-rekordhoehe-li.10019627) |

## Context: Digitalization of Filmmaking
| Date | Event | Relevance for our project | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2002-05-16 | "Star Wars: Episode II" is the first major Hollywood film shot with digital cameras | Beginning of the shift from film to digital. | [BFI](https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-early-digital-films) |
| 2013 / 2016 | From 2013, most top films are shot digitally; from 2016, over 90% | Analog becomes the exception. Relevant for our side question on the share of films shot on film. | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_films_shot_on_digital_video_prior_to_2012) |

## Phasen für die Analyse

- **Before streaming (until 2013):** reference period
- **Streaming era (2014-2019):** Amazon Prime und Netflix launch
- **COVID-19 (2020-2021):** marked separately in the analysis
- **Recovery (from 2022):** comparision with the pre-COVID period
